JG Project Log is JUMPINGGOOSE's internal tool for organising project allocations, recording employee work and reviewing project performance.

Sign in with Google

What the platform does

  • Tracks projects and their assignments across Pond 1, Pond 2 and Pool.
  • Lets approved employees and interns record work dates, projects, activities, task notes and time spent.
  • Gives authorised managers access to team activity and project reporting.
  • Restricts salary, revenue and profitability information to explicitly authorised management accounts.

Who can access it?

JG Project Log is not a public sign-up service. Google sign-in is used to verify identity, and application access is limited to active, approved JUMPINGGOOSE team members, interns and authorised collaborators.

Having a Google account alone does not grant access.

Your information

We use account-identification information for sign-in, and work-related information to operate the project and time-tracking system. For more information about processing, storage, access and requests, read our Privacy Policy.
