Privacy Policy

This policy explains how JUMPINGGOOSE uses information in JG Project Log, an internal project-management and time-tracking application.

Effective date: 3 November 2026

1. Operator and scope

JG Project Log is operated by JUMPINGGOOSE for its authorised personnel, interns and collaborators. This policy covers information handled through the Project Log application at projectlog.jumpinggoose.com.

2. Information we process

  • Sign-in identity: the Google account email address and basic identity information supplied during Google sign-in, used to authenticate an account and check whether it is authorised.
  • Team profile: name, work email, group or Pond, role, employment type, access level and relevant team status.
  • Work-log information: work dates, projects, activities, task descriptions, time spent and records of changes.
  • Project and business information: project assignments, planned effort, revenue and costs. Salary and profitability records are accessible only to authorised management accounts.
  • Technical and security information: authentication-session and service information required for secure operation, which may also be processed by the application's infrastructure providers.

3. How Google account information is used

JG Project Log uses Google sign-in through Supabase Authentication to verify a user's identity and associate their session with an approved application account. Google account information is used for authentication and access control, not for advertising or building public user profiles.

Google sign-in does not, by itself, authorise access to employee records or financial information. The application checks its own approved-user records and access rules.

4. Why we use information

  • To operate the internal project-allocation and time-tracking system.
  • To record work completed against projects and internal activities.
  • To support resourcing, operational reporting and authorised financial analysis.
  • To maintain account security, manage access permissions and investigate technical issues.
  • To meet legitimate business recordkeeping and applicable legal obligations.

5. Storage, service providers and sharing

JG Project Log uses Supabase for its application database and authentication and Vercel to host the website. Encrypted database backups are stored using the project's GitHub Actions backup workflow. These providers process information to support operation, security and recovery of the application.

Within JUMPINGGOOSE, access depends on a person's assigned role. Work logs and team activity may be viewed by authorised managers; compensation and project financials are restricted more narrowly. Information is not intended for public disclosure or sale to advertisers.

6. Retention and access changes

Work and project records may be retained for operational history, financial review, business continuity and applicable legal or contractual requirements. Disabling an account or marking a team member as exited does not necessarily remove historical project and work records. Backup copies may remain for a limited period under the system's backup-retention settings.

Retention and deletion requests are assessed in light of organisational and legal requirements.

7. Accuracy, access and requests

Authorised users can review and, where permitted, correct their work-log entries in the application. For account access, corrections, questions about personal information, or deletion requests, contact your JUMPINGGOOSE project administrator through the usual organisational contact channel.

If you do not know the relevant administrator, contact JUMPINGGOOSE through its official website.

8. Security

Google authentication, application-level role checks, database row-level security rules and encrypted backups are used to protect information. No online system can guarantee absolute security; suspected unauthorised access should be reported promptly to the JUMPINGGOOSE administrator.

9. Changes to this policy

JUMPINGGOOSE may update this policy as the application or its practices change. The latest version will be available on this page with an updated effective date.
